# Master prompt: 8 Meta video ads (ViralFrames x4, Headcount AI x4)

This is the owner's voice brief (15 Nov 2026), rewritten as a production brief. Every concept writer, scriptwriter, motion designer and builder reads all of it before making anything. Stage 1 (this round): 10 storyboarded concepts per product. The owner picks 4 per product. Stage 2: build those 8.

## 1. The job

Make 60 second (or shorter) 9:16 video ads for Meta Reels, Stories and feed that a cold Indian founder, marketer or creator watches to the end and then taps. Each ad must:

1. Stop the scroll in the first second with a bold, specific, value-led claim, shown and spoken at once.
2. Feel like news: something that just launched and that the viewer has not seen on the internet before.
3. Show the product working, and give the viewer one clear "aha" moment they can picture in their own business.
4. Stack the value: what they get, what it replaces, what it costs, and the bonuses.
5. Create honest FOMO (launch price, the real deadline, the cost of waiting) and end on a call to action that pushes hard.

Salesy means value-driven, never pushy. The viewer should feel they would be silly not to tap.

## 2. Who watches

Cold Meta traffic, India first, 90% on phones, most with sound off at first. D2C founders, small agency owners, solo marketers, creators, coaches and SaaS founders. They already pay for ChatGPT or Claude. They are tired of salaries, retainers, per-video invoices, editors who say "first cut by Friday", and AI tools that hand back prompts instead of finished work. Awareness: problem-aware to solution-aware. Lead with the cost they hate or the outcome they want, then the mechanism, then the price.

## 4. Hard rules (a violation means rebuild)
- No em dashes or en dashes anywhere, on screen or in copy.
- Never the word "Shopify". Say "your store" or "ecommerce store".
- Prices in ₹. Spoken prices in words; the figure always on screen.
- Never mention refunds, guarantees or money back.
- No invented testimonials, customer counts, ratings, revenue, ROAS or "as seen in". Bold claims come from price contrast, countables and product truth only. Results inside a mock say "example".
- Real urgency only: Headcount AI "₹2,999 till 30 Nov, then ₹4,999". ViralFrames "launch price ₹2,999, regular ₹5,999" with no date.
- ChatGPT, Claude and Codex appear only as "runs inside". No real people's faces, no celebrities. AI footage of people is faceless or anonymous (hands, over-shoulder, silhouettes).
- No street address, no WhatsApp number.

## 5. Narration (the owner's top priority)
- Register: a university professor explaining something to one person. Complete sentences, one idea at a time, cause then effect, numbers with their conditions. Warm, curious, confident. Contractions, small human turns ("Now here's the thing", "Let me show you").
- Never clipped ad-speak, never stacked slogans, never fragments like "Ghosts: billed." Never AI-sounding filler: game-changer, revolutionise, unlock, seamless, elevate, supercharge, "in today's world".
- Length: 150 to 175 spoken words for a 57 to 60 s ad, about 2.8 to 3.0 words a second. Cut ideas, not grammar, to fit.
- First word by 0.3 s. The hook line is spoken and shown at the same time.
- Voice: ElevenLabs v3 on Replicate (the engine behind the 14 approved ViralFrames films), with v3 audio tags for natural breath, emphasis and small pauses. One narrator per product, cast from 2 samples with the pilot. Clear Indian-English or neutral international accent, the owner's pick.

## 6. Visual and edit craft
- 1080x1920, 30 fps, 57 to 60 s. Key text inside the safe zone: top 14% and bottom 35% clear of anything important (Reels UI).
- Built in HyperFrames (HTML to video) at portfolio grade: one designed world per ad, a continuous camera, z-depth, match cuts and push-throughs that carry meaning, kinetic type as a character. No flat card pops on a coloured field.
- Not rapid-fire. Shots hold 3 to 6 s so the viewer understands. Something always moves (camera drift, type building, UI acting), nothing sits still more than 1 s while the voice talks.
- Real product beats: Claude desktop and ChatGPT windows as pixel-true HTML replicas; real ViralFrames output clips; real Headcount AI mocks. Never AI-generated UI.
- AI video clips only where live action adds feeling (a founder's desk at 2 a.m., a morning desk): Seedance 2.0 or Veo on Replicate, faceless, natural grade, no brand tint.
- Word-synced burned-in captions in every ad (sound-off viewing), key numbers highlighted.
- Music: modern, warm, mid-tempo (110 to 120 BPM), on from frame 0, VO-forward mix (bed x0.67, 10% dip under speech, -16 LUFS, limiter -1 dB). Sparse UI sound cues, no impacts or risers.
- End card (last 5 to 7 s): product lockup, struck price and price, the deadline for Headcount AI, and a big animated button with a tap, placed above the bottom 35%. Meta's own CTA button: Shop Now (or Get Offer).
- Where it fits, a ViralFrames ad is itself made in one of the pack's own styles, with a small "Made with ViralFrames" tag. That tag is used only when it is true.

## 7. Every ad's spine (adapt, do not template)
1. 0 to 4 s, Hook: a pattern-breaking visual and a bold, specific claim, shown and spoken.
2. 4 to 15 s, Stakes: the cost or pain in the viewer's own money and time.
3. 15 to 30 s, Mechanism and product preview: what it is and where it runs.
4. By 35 s, the aha: one concrete example the viewer can picture for their business. Any open loop from the hook pays off here.
5. 35 to 50 s, Value stack: what it replaces, price contrast, bonuses.
6. 50 to 60 s, FOMO and CTA: launch price or deadline, the button, one closing line tied to the ad's idea.

## 8. Per ad deliverables (stage 2)
`final/<ID>.mp4` (1080x1920), `<ID>.srt`, a 4:5 cut if asked, a thumbnail frame, and Meta copy in `copy.csv` (primary text 2 to 4 short lines with the hook first, headline up to 40 chars, description up to 30, CTA button). A QA note per ad: words per second, longest still, safe-zone check, every on-screen word read, rule grep (dashes, "Shopify", refund, guarantee).

## 9. Build order
Pilot first: one ViralFrames and one Headcount AI ad are built in parallel, watched end to end, and sent to the owner. After his notes, the other six are built by parallel sub-agents, each working from this brief, its approved storyboard and the pilot's fixes.
