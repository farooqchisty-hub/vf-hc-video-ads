1

[Group] watching AI do their entire job (despair-reaction meme)

9/10

First 3 s: 0 to 3 s: a famous-looking group shot of professionals frozen in silent despair or disbelief (couch slump, trading floor staring at a screen, villain's dead stare), slow push-in, no dialogue. The joke lives entirely in the one-line caption naming the group: "marketing agencies watching Claude do their entire job 😭".

Why it works: Instant in-group/out-group read: the viewer is either the group (fear) or the person replacing them (glee). Zero setup needed, the caption does the work, and it names a concrete job the product replaces. On X it is a live template right now: 8 posts in 6 weeks reusing the exact wording, best one 725k views.

How we make it (legal): Every viral version uses film/TV footage (thumbnails show Wolf of Wall Street, The Boys/Homelander, Iron Man, Breaking Bad). Not usable in a paid ad. Lawful look-alike: shoot it ourselves or hire 3 to 4 actors: a 'marketing agency' team in blazers on an office couch or around one laptop, all staring at the screen in silent horror, slow phone push-in, cool office light. Stock fallback: Pexels/Mixkit searches 'shocked office team looking at laptop', 'business people disappointed watching screen'. Keep it silent or use a licensed ambient sting.
