Step 2. The next set: 25 ads in two waves

Every ad sells the whole team, an edge over competitors, or Claude with a team behind it. Single agents appear only as proof inside that frame.

Wave 1 · 17 ads

Fast, from what we already have

Tests hook lines cheaply. Winning lines become the hooks of the Wave 2 films.

4 short cuts of the winners, 20 to 25 seconds

HC-A-25, HC-N-25, HC-D-25, HC-F-25. Same hook, the single strongest proof beat, the close and the CTA tag. Re-edited from the existing masters and voice takes, no new generation. Each goes into the same ad set as its long version, so Meta picks the length. Every live Headcount ad today is 38 to 48 seconds; short is untested.

12 statics, 4 angles x 3 lines

In the approved organic look: the site's own fonts and colours, one plain line plus an italic twist, real product UI. 4:5 for feed and 9:16 for stories and reels.

1 carousel: Meet your 40

10 cards. Cover: "Your marketing team is ready. Swipe to meet them." Then 8 department cards, each with one flagship agent and its real finished output. Last card: Meet your 40 agents. Shows the whole team in one swipe, which is what the winning ads sell.

Step 3. Test setup

Winners, ₹8,500 a day (existing campaigns)

  • HC-A-v2 + HC-A-25: ₹3,000 a day
  • HC-N-v2 + HC-N-25: ₹2,500 a day
  • HC-D-v2 + HC-D-25: ₹1,000 a day
  • HC-F-v2 + HC-F-25: ₹1,000 a day
  • HC-L-v2: ₹1,000 a day

Round 4 test campaign, ₹5,000 a day

  • Wave 1: 4 static ad sets (one per angle, 3 statics each) and 1 carousel ad set, ₹1,000 a day each
  • Wave 2: each film gets its own ad set at ₹1,000 a day, taking the slot of a Wave 1 loser
  • Same settings as today: Purchase on the shared pixel, buyer exclusion on

Read and act rules, checked at the two daily windows only (09:15 and 17:15 IST)

  • Hook passes (statics): link click rate at least 2.5% and at most ₹70 per visit after ₹1,500 spent.
  • Pause: ₹3,000 spent with 0 checkouts started, or ₹6,000 spent with 0 buyers, or above ₹6,000 per buyer after ₹9,000 spent.
  • Scale: 2 or more buyers at ₹3,000 per buyer or less. Raise that ad set 30%, at most once every 2 days.
  • Count buyers from real orders, not Meta (Meta over-counts by about 40%). Ad name in lowercase goes in utm_content, for example hc-o, hc-a-25, hc-st-edge-2.

Rules every ad follows

  • Any business. Never "for D2C" or "for ecommerce brands".
  • Say agents, never prompts. Claude before ChatGPT in hooks.
  • No dates and no deadline in any ad, so nothing needs a re-cut when the launch date rolls.
  • Price only as the ₹75-an-agent unit line (HC-A static 2, HC-R close). Nothing about refunds or guarantees.
  • Real product UI. Example brands labelled "Example". No invented results, no fake testimonials, presenters speak as explainers.
  • Films: emoji hook on frame 1, first word by 0.3 seconds, one unbroken voice take per beat, content format first, product enters about a third in.

One landing page lever

The edge ads point to one agent ("Meet Competitor Watch") but land on the general hero. A link like /headcount/?agent=competitor-watch that opens the showcase on that agent would match the ad to the page. Cheap to build. It also ties into the Clarity and order re-read due about 25 Nov.
